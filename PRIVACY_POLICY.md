# Privacy Policy for Mahe Delivery

**Last Updated**: January 2026

## 1. Introduction

Mahe Delivery ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you about how we look after your personal data when you use our mobile application and tell you about your privacy rights.

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Phone number, name, email address (optional)
- **Profile Information**: Profile picture, delivery addresses
- **Order Information**: Food orders, delivery preferences, payment information
- **Communications**: Customer support messages, feedback

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Location Data**: GPS location for delivery tracking and restaurant discovery
- **Usage Data**: App interactions, features used, time spent
- **Log Data**: Error logs, crash reports, performance data

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Firebase Services**: Authentication data, analytics data
- **Payment Providers**: Transaction data (we don't store credit card details)
- **Map Services**: Location and navigation data

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:
- Provide and maintain our services
- Process your food orders and deliveries
- Enable real-time order tracking
- Process payments securely
- Send order updates and notifications
- Improve our app and services
- Provide customer support
- Comply with legal obligations
- Prevent fraud and abuse

## 4. Data Sharing

We share your data only when necessary:

### 4.1 With Vendors
- Order details and delivery address
- Contact information for order-related communication

### 4.2 With Riders
- Delivery address and contact information
- Order details for delivery

### 4.3 With Service Providers
- Firebase (authentication, database, analytics)
- Payment processors (transaction processing)
- Map services (location and navigation)

### 4.4 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your data if required by law or to:
- Comply with legal processes
- Protect our rights and property
- Prevent fraud or security issues

## 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures including:
- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Firebase security rules for access control
- Regular security audits
- Secure authentication mechanisms

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Object to data processing
- Data portability
- Withdraw consent

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@mahedelivery.com

## 7. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary:
- Account data: Until account deletion
- Order history: 2 years after last order
- Analytics data: 26 months
- Log data: 90 days

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Location Data

We collect location data to:
- Show nearby restaurants
- Enable delivery tracking
- Calculate delivery fees

You can disable location services in your device settings, but this will limit app functionality.

## 10. Cookies and Tracking

We use:
- Firebase Analytics for app usage insights
- Crash reporting tools for bug fixes
- Performance monitoring for optimization

You can opt out of analytics in app settings.

## 11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside Ethiopia. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We'll notify you of significant changes through the app or email.
